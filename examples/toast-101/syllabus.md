# TOAST 101: Foundations of Applied Toast Studies

*A fictional syllabus, made up to demo the syllabus-to-course skill.*

**Department of Breakfast Sciences** · 3 credits · Fall semester
**Instructor:** Prof. B. Crumb · Office hours: whenever the toaster pops
**Prerequisites:** Bread, Introductory (BREAD 100), or the ability to open a bag of sliced bread

## Course description
Students will develop a rigorous understanding of toast, from the chemistry of browning to the ethics of the burnt slice. The course culminates in a capstone breakfast presented to a panel of hungry peers.

## Weekly schedule

| Week | Topic | Reading |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The Science of Brown: Maillard reaction vs. caramelization | Crumb, *Toast: A Love Story*, Ch. 1 |
| 2 | Bread Selection: crumb structure, moisture, and staling | Ch. 2–3 |
| 3 | The Toaster: heating elements, timers, and the pop-up mechanism | Ch. 4 |
| 4 | Doneness: shades of brown and how to measure them | Ch. 5 |
| 5 | Butter Dynamics: melting, spreading, and the torn-slice problem | Ch. 6 |
| 6 | Midterm (practical): produce three identical slices | — |
| 7 | Toppings I: sweet | Ch. 7 |
| 8 | Toppings II: savory | Ch. 8 |
| 9 | Failure Analysis: burnt toast, soggy toast, and the fire alarm | Ch. 9 |
| 10 | Capstone breakfast | — |

## Grading
- Weekly toast logs: 30%
- Midterm practical: 30%
- Capstone breakfast: 40%

## Policies
Late toast is cold toast and will be graded accordingly.
