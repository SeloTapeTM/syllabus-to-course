# Wrap-up templates (Phase 4)

## Final review quiz
- 10-20 questions, weighted toward the objectives that were hardest or most central to the user's goal.
- Mix question types: recall, application, and at least two that combine ideas from different modules.
- Tag each question with its module (e.g. "[M3]") so the user knows what to revisit.
- Put answers and explanations after all the questions.

## Capstone project
```markdown
# Capstone: [Title]
**Goal link:** [how this proves the user's stated goal]
**Uses modules:** [list]
**Deliverable:** [concrete artifact: report, program, analysis, design]
**Milestones:** 1. ... 2. ... 3. ... (each with an hour estimate)
**Done when:** [3-5 checkable criteria, e.g. a self-grading rubric]
**Stretch:** [optional]
```
If the syllabus has a final project, base the capstone on it and adapt it to the user's goal.

## One-page cheat sheet
- Fits on one printed page: dense, scannable, no prose paragraphs.
- Group by module. Include key definitions, formulas or syntax, decision rules ("use X when..."), and the common mistakes the quizzes revealed.
- Offer it as an export: Word (docx skill), PDF, or an HTML artifact.
