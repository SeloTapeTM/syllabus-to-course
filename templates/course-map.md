# Course map template

Fill this in during Phase 2. Keep it to one screen if the course allows.

```markdown
# [Course title]: Self-Study Map

**Your profile:** [level] · [N] h/week · Goal: [goal]
**Length:** [M] modules over about [W] weeks · about [T] hours total

| # | Module | Syllabus source | Topics | Est. hours | Practice task (maps to) |
|---|--------|-----------------|--------|-----------:|-------------------------|
| 1 | [name] | Week 1 | [topic, topic] | 4 | [task] (Problem Set 1) |
| 2 | ... | Weeks 2-3 (merged) | ... | 6 | ... |

**Assessment mapping**
- Midterm → Module [k] review quiz
- Final project → Phase 4 capstone

**Gaps in the syllabus** (I'll ask before filling these)
- Week [n]: "[topic]" has no description or readings

**Optional extensions** (outside the syllabus; skip freely)
- [extension]: [why it helps your goal]

Approve this map, or tell me what to change (pace, order, depth, skip a module).
```

Hour estimates: lesson reading (about 30-45 min per major topic) + assigned textbook reading (about 10-15 pages/hour for dense technical text) + practice (usually the largest share) + quiz (15 min). Scale to the weekly budget rather than the original course's credit hours.
